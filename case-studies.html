CASE STUDY 01

Strengthening Billing Adjustment Controls

  • Control matrix
  • Exception review
  • Approval evidence
  • Audit readiness
01 / THE PROBLEM

Manual credits and billing adjustments can reduce revenue without a consistent approval trail, creating financial risk and audit cleanup work.

02 / THE APPROACH

Define control rules for high-dollar adjustments, missing approval notes, repeated corrections by the same user, and stale review items. Turn those rules into an exception review cadence with owners and evidence requirements.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Finance gains a clearer approval trail, managers know which adjustments need review, and audit support becomes easier because exceptions are tracked with ownership.

CASE STUDY 02

Turning Data Quality Findings Into Action

  • Stakeholder brief
  • Owner matrix
  • Issue tracker
  • Business impact summary
01 / THE PROBLEM

Finance, operations, and IT can look at the same data issue differently, which slows resolution when ownership and business impact are unclear.

02 / THE APPROACH

Translate technical findings into department-specific actions: finance receives exposure and control impact, operations receives process patterns, and IT receives system mapping or integration evidence.

03 / THE OUTCOME

Teams leave with the same evidence, clear ownership, and a follow-up plan instead of debating separate spreadsheets or symptoms.

CASE STUDY 03

Automating Recurring Operational Reports

  • Stored procedures
  • Scheduled jobs
  • Run logs
  • PITR awareness
01 / THE PROBLEM

Manual weekly reports waste analyst time and increase the chance of inconsistent calculations.

02 / THE APPROACH

Move repeatable report logic into stored procedures, schedule the reporting job, log each run, and add checks for unusual totals or row counts.

03 / THE OUTCOME

The business gets faster reporting, fewer manual steps, and better visibility when something in the data changes unexpectedly.
