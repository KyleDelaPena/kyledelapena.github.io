A forecast dashboard that brings weather, water conditions, and estimated lunar activity windows into one view.

  • Expo
  • React Native
  • TypeScript
  • Open-Meteo
  • SunCalc

The idea

Planning time on the water often means comparing weather, marine readings, and activity windows across several websites.

The implementation

  • Location search & saved spots
  • Weather & marine forecasts
  • 24-hour fishing clock
